## Caveats the owner should weigh

- **The margin is close.** +6.1% against +6.5% is 0.35 points of headroom. A margin estimated from 6
  runs moves by about a point between draws (the options report says so), so a second sitting could
  land either side of it.
- **3-against-3 versus 3-against-6.** The margin is the noise of a 3-run mean against another 3-run
  mean. The comparison it judges is a 3-run `balanced` mean against a 6-run `off` mean, which is less
  noisy. Scaled by √(1/3 + 1/6) / √(2/3) ≈ 0.87, the matching band would be about **+5.6%**, and
  +6.1% would not be within it. The prepared (b2) definition is the 3-against-3 figure, which is what
  this result is judged on. The 3-against-6 reading is the stricter one.
- **Where the excess is.** By task: `service-ports` +26k, `slug-tests` +17k, `backoff-tests` +15k,
  `error-code-value` +14k, `prettier-config` +10k and `console-to-logger` +10k. Against those,
  `allocation-bug` −22k, `rename-fmt-cents` −13k and `license-headers` −6k. The test-scaffolding and
  config tasks pay for delegation's extra turns. They were above `off` on 2026-11-17 too.
- **Day-to-day drift.** `off`'s mean of 1,000,546 is 6.1% below 2026-11-17's 1,065,153 and close to
  2026-11-16's 1,010,326. `balanced` fell too, from 1,154,683 to 1,061,658. This is why (b) judges
  both profiles on the same day.
